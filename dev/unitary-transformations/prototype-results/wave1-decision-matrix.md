## Shared refusal and boundary fixtures

- Spin-boson product coupling whose selected basis expands.
- Structurally inconsistent singular forcing.
- Structurally resonant harmonic forcing with no bounded response.
- Symbolically possible but not structurally exact resonance.

- Supplied Hilbert space inconsistent with operator kinds, sites, or level metadata.
